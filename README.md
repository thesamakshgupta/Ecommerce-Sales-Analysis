1. Project Title / Headline

**E-Commerce Analytics: Sales & Profit Insights Dashboard**

An interactive business intelligence dashboard built to analyze e-commerce sales performance, profitability, product trends, and customer purchasing behavior. This project transforms raw transactional data into meaningful business insights using Python, MySQL, SQL, and Power BI.

2. Short Description / Purpose

The E-Commerce Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard is an end-to-end data analytics project designed to understand business performance through data-driven insights.

The dashboard helps analyze total sales, profit, order volume, product performance, regional trends, and customer payment preferences. It provides an interactive environment to explore business metrics, identify growth opportunities, and support better decision-making.

3. Tech Stack

The project was built using the following tools and technologies:

- 🐍 **Python** – Used for data cleaning, preprocessing, and feature engineering.
- 📊 **Pandas & NumPy** – Used for data manipulation and numerical analysis.
- 🗄️ **MySQL** – Used to store and manage the cleaned dataset.
- 🔗 **SQLAlchemy** – Used to establish database connectivity between Python and MySQL.
- 📝 **SQL** – Used for business analysis, aggregation, and querying.
- 📈 **Power BI Desktop** – Main platform for interactive dashboard development.
- 🧠 **DAX** – Used to create measures such as Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, and Average Order Value.

4. Data Source

**Source:** E-Commerce Transaction Dataset (Excel)

The dataset contains transactional information, including order details, product names, quantities, unit prices, discounts, net amounts, profits, customer locations, payment methods, and order statuses.

The raw dataset was cleaned and transformed using Python, exported as a CSV file, and integrated into a MySQL database for further analysis and visualization in Power BI.

 5. Features / Highlights

• Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate large amounts of transactional data, making it difficult to track sales performance, identify profitable products, understand customer preferences, and monitor operational efficiency.

Analyzing this data helps businesses make informed decisions regarding sales strategies, product performance, and customer satisfaction.

• Goal of the Dashboard

To develop an interactive dashboard that provides a comprehensive overview of e-commerce performance, identifies key business trends, and supports data-driven decision-making through dynamic visualizations.

• Walkthrough of Key Visuals

- **KPI Cards:** Display Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, and Average Order Value to provide a quick overview of business performance.
- **Monthly Sales & Profit Trends:** A line chart to analyze revenue and profitability over time and identify seasonal patterns.
- **Top-Selling Products:** A bar chart highlighting products with the highest sales contribution.
- **Regional Sales Analysis:** Compare sales performance across different cities and regions to identify high-performing markets.
- **Payment Method Distribution:** A donut chart showing customer payment preferences.
- **Order Status Analysis:** Analyze delivered, cancelled, and other order statuses to monitor operational performance.
- **Interactive Slicers:** Filter the dashboard by city, date, and payment mode for detailed and dynamic analysis.

• Business Impact & Insights

- **Sales Optimization:** Identify top-performing products and categories to improve sales strategies.
- **Profitability Analysis:** Understand profit contribution and evaluate overall business performance.
- **Regional Analysis:** Identify high-performing cities and potential market expansion opportunities.
- **Customer Behavior:** Analyze payment preferences and purchasing patterns to improve customer experience.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Monitor cancelled orders and identify potential business challenges.
- **Strategic Decision-Making:** Use interactive visualizations to uncover trends and support data-driven business decisions.
